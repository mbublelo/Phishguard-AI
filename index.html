Six checks, one verdict

Every submission runs through the same pipeline a Tier 1 SOC analyst would follow — automated, and fast enough to run on every inbound message.

Sender & SPF/DKIM checks

Validates authentication records and flags spoofed or misaligned sending domains.

URL reputation

Cross-references every link against known malicious and newly-registered domain lists.

OSINT domain intelligence

Pulls WHOIS age, hosting history and prior abuse reports for every linked domain.

Header integrity
